# Plan 29-02: Model capex opex growth and cost-cut decisions

<objective>
Turn the inventory and pricing evidence into a decision-useful `SPEND.md`
report with capex/opex framing, growth scenarios, cost-cut options, and Heroku
migration thresholds.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- T-29-04: Decision bias from omitting operational labor or migration risk.
  Mitigation: include capex-style engineering/operator costs and unknowns.
- T-29-05: Unsafe cost cuts that weaken reliability. Mitigation: every
  recommendation must include operational risk and validation requirements.
- T-29-06: Personalized traffic or cache misses understated. Mitigation:
  scenario model must include cache hit ratio and authenticated traffic.
</threat_model>
